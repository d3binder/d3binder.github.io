FIRE Nate - Social Security & Pension Bridge

You don't have to fund every retirement year yourself.

The standard "25x expenses" rule assumes your portfolio covers 100% of your spending forever. But almost every early retiree gets Social Security or a pension eventually — which means your real number is usually smaller, once it's split into the years you self-fund and the years you're supplemented.

Phase 1 · Self-funded gap

From the day you retire until Social Security (or a pension) starts, your portfolio covers 100% of expenses on its own — a fixed number of years, not forever.

Phase 2 · Supplemented

Once benefits kick in, your portfolio only needs to cover the gap between your expenses and your benefit — and that ongoing amount is what actually needs to last indefinitely.
