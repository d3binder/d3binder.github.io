FIRE Nate - Coast & Barista FIRE

You might not need to save another dollar.

Coast FI is the point where your existing savings, left completely alone to grow, will reach your retirement goal by your target age — no more contributions required. Once you're there, "Barista FIRE" means downshifting to lower-stress, lower-income work that just covers your bills while your nest egg keeps compounding in the background.

Coast FI number

The amount you'd need today to hit your goal by retirement on growth alone — found by discounting your goal backward from your retirement age at your expected return rate.

Coast FI age

If you're not there yet, this projects your current contributions forward to find the first age where your growing balance crosses above the (shrinking) amount you'd need to coast from that age.

Barista phase

From your chosen Barista start age onward, contributions drop to whatever a lighter income can still spare — often just enough to cover living costs, sometimes a little more. The balance still grows toward your goal on its own.

This assumes a steady annual return with no volatility, which real markets never deliver — treat the Coast FI age as a rough milestone, not a guarantee, and revisit it as your numbers change. It also doesn't account for inflation eroding your goal's purchasing power over a long coast period; consider using a goal already adjusted for that.
