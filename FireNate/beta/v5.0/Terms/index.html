4. Your data

FireNate has no user accounts and no server-side database. Every value you type into a calculator is saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device, so your calculators still have your last inputs the next time you visit. Nothing you enter is transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by this site.

A few features let you move your own data around, entirely at your own initiative:

  • The Profile Manager can export your saved profiles and calculator inputs to a JSON file you download and keep yourself.
  • Individual calculators can generate a "shareable link" that encodes your current inputs directly in the URL, so you can send a specific scenario to someone else without either of you needing an account.

Clearing your browser's site data, or using the "Clear all local data" option in Settings, permanently deletes everything stored on that device. There's no backup copy anywhere else unless you exported or shared one yourself.
