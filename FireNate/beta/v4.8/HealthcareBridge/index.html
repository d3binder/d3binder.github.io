FIRE Nate - Healthcare & ACA Bridge

The gap nobody budgets for: health insurance before 65.

Retire before Medicare eligibility and you're buying coverage on the ACA marketplace — where the subsidy you get depends entirely on your Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI), not your net worth. That makes MAGI management (Roth conversion timing, capital gains harvesting, what you sell and when) a real lever on your healthcare bill.

Bridge years · Pre-Medicare

From retirement until age 65, you're buying ACA marketplace coverage — and your premium tax credit is based on MAGI as a percentage of the Federal Poverty Level (FPL), recalculated every year.

Age 65+ · Medicare

Medicare eligibility starts at 65, ending the bridge. This calculator doesn't model Medicare Part B/D premiums or IRMAA surcharges — only the marketplace years before it.

Subsidy figures here are simplified and illustrative — the applicable-percentage brackets and FPL amounts are editable assumptions, not live IRS or HHS figures. Confirm current-year numbers at healthcare.gov before making decisions. Below roughly 100% of FPL, marketplace subsidies generally aren't available at all — eligibility instead depends on your state's Medicaid expansion status, which this calculator doesn't model.
