1. Minimums first

Every debt gets at least its minimum payment every month, so nothing falls behind.

2. Smallest balance next

Every extra dollar goes toward the debt with the smallest balance — not the highest rate. Fast wins build momentum.

3. Snowball it

Once a debt is gone, its whole payment rolls into the next one — so the amount you can throw at each debt keeps growing.
