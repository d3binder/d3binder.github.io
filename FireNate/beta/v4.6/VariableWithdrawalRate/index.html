FIRE Nate - Variable Withdrawal & Cash Buffer

Real retirees don't stay the course in a crash.

A rigid fixed withdrawal has to assume the worst historical sequence of returns. Guardrails, spending cuts, and a cash buffer are how real retirees actually manage sequence-of-returns risk — this stress-tests those strategies against every real market cycle since 1928, the same dataset the plain Safe Withdrawal Rate calculator uses.

Guardrails

Each year, if your withdrawal has drifted to 20% above your starting rate (the portfolio shrank), spending gets cut 10%. Drift 20% below it (the portfolio grew), and spending gets raised 10%.

Spending cuts

A simpler alternative: when your portfolio falls a set amount below its peak, cut spending by a fixed percentage until it recovers.

Cash buffer

Hold 1–3 years of spending in cash, set aside from the portfolio. In a down year, spend from cash instead of selling stocks at a loss; in an up year, refill the buffer from gains.

Guardrail bands (±20%) and adjustment size (10%) follow the commonly-cited Guyton-Klinger parameters and aren't separately adjustable here. "Bear-Market Cut" and "Drawdown Trigger" only apply when "Cut spending on drawdown" is selected. The cash buffer works alongside either strategy — set it to 0 years to turn it off.
