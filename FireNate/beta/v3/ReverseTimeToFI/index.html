How the math works

Why 4%?

The 4% rule comes from historical market research into sustainable withdrawal rates in retirement. Researchers such as William Bengen studied decades of stock and bond returns across many overlapping 30-year stretches, including recessions and downturns, and found that withdrawing about 4% of a portfolio in the first year — then adjusting for inflation each year after — held up in nearly all of them.

It's a useful planning anchor, not a guarantee. Markets, taxes, and your own spending will move around it, so treat the number this calculator gives you as a floor to plan from, not a promise.

Good to know

This tool assumes a constant inflation rate and a constant return on investments every year until retirement. Real markets don't move in a straight line — use this as a directional plan, and revisit it every year or two.
