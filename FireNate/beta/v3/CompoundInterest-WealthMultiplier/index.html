02 — Year after year

The power of compound interest

Now invest that amount every single year for 60 years. The red sliver is what you actually put in. Everything sage-green above it is interest the money earned on its own.

Amount invested Compounding interest
Total invested over 60 years: $93,600
Total interest earned: $5,114,145
Grand total, invested + interest: $5,207,745
The power of time in the market. Invest this amount every year and let it grow at your rate—this is what compounding alone builds for you.
The cost of waiting. This much smaller pile is all you'd have if you simply saved the same dollars in a drawer for 60 years—no growth at all.
