  • F.A.Q. page: "Other Common Topics & Questions" category renamed to "Other Topics," and a new "Will FireNate always be free?" question added
  • Glossary: merged two overlapping categories ("Early access to retirement funds" and "Early retirement gaps") into a single "Early Retirement" section
  • Renamed "Picking Calculators" to "Calculator Picker" site-wide (homepage, hamburger menu, Getting Started, and the F.A.Q.)
  • Moved Compound Interest & Wealth Multiplier's saved inputs off a cookie and onto localStorage, and gave Loan Calculator and Homes vs. Stocks their own localStorage persistence (with a "Last saved" indicator) — both previously reset every time you reloaded
  • Registered all three of the above in the shared saved-data list, and filled in the last gaps in Profile Manager's example-data generator (it was missing sample data for Cash Out or Hold, Compound Interest, Loan Calculator, and Homes vs. Stocks)
