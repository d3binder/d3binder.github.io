FireNate — Getting Started

New here? Let's get you set up.

A few minutes now saves you from re-typing the same numbers on every calculator later. Here's the fastest path to getting real value out of this site.

New to FIRE? Terms like Coast FIRE, MAGI, and the 4% rule come up throughout this site. The Glossary has plain-English definitions for all of them — worth a quick look before diving in below.

It's also always one tap away — find it under Resources in the hamburger menu, top-right of every page.

01

Set up your profile

Your info — the icon in the top-right of every page — holds the handful of numbers that power the whole site: your age, annual income, current savings, savings goal, target retirement age, and expected return rate. Fill it in once and every calculator auto-fills from it, so you're never re-entering the same numbers twice.

02

Pick a calculator to start with

Not sure where to begin? These three cover the most common questions — start with whichever matches what you're trying to figure out.

FI Snapshot
Your financial-independence status at a glance — age, savings, goal progress, and whether you've hit Coast-FI.
Time to FI
"How many years until I'm financially independent?" — see every savings rate from 0% to 100%.
Reverse Time to FI
Start from the freedom date you want, and work backward to what you need to save each week.
03

Save a snapshot before you experiment

About to try a different return rate, retirement age, or goal? Click the camera icon next to "Your info" first — it saves a dated snapshot of your current numbers, including whatever you've entered on every calculator page, not just your profile. That way "what if" never means losing your real plan.

Made a mistake and cleared your info by accident? The trash icon automatically backs up your profile right before it clears it, so you can load it right back.

04

Compare plans in Profile Manager

Profile Manager is where every saved snapshot lives — name them, load any one back into your live profile, delete the ones you don't need, and see each one's stats side by side. You can also export all your saved profiles to a file, and import them back in later or on another device.

Want to load a saved snapshot without leaving the page you're on? The "Load saved profile" icon — the people icon next to the camera — opens a quick list of every snapshot you've saved, right there in "Your info."

Just want to send one specific scenario to someone, without a whole file? Each page's "Your info" panel also has a share-link icon — it copies a link that encodes your current numbers, ready to paste anywhere.

05

Your data stays on your device

Nothing you enter is sent anywhere — everything is saved locally in your browser. Use the gear icon to switch between light and dark mode, or to wipe everything and start over with "Clear all local data" if you're ever handing off this device or just want a clean slate.

A few habits that pay off

  • Snapshot before you tinker. A quick camera-icon click means you can always get back to your real numbers.
  • Name your snapshots something you'll recognize later — "Conservative 6% return" beats "Snapshot Aug 14."
  • Export a backup every so often. It's a plain JSON file you can keep anywhere, and it restores everything — profile, snapshots, and every calculator's own inputs.
  • Revisit your profile as things change. A raise, a new goal, or a birthday all shift the math — keeping it current keeps every calculator honest.
